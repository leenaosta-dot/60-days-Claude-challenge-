Day 12 - Career Applications with Claude

Challenge Objective

Built a complete Job Search and Personal Branding Toolkit using Claude AI.

What I Did

- Uploaded my resume to Claude.
- Selected target roles in education and academic leadership.
- Analyzed a relevant job description.
- Generated an ATS-friendly cover letter.
- Created recruiter and hiring manager outreach emails.
- Created LinkedIn networking and referral messages.
- Generated interview introduction and talking points.
- Reviewed skill gap analysis and career growth opportunities.
- Developed a personal branding statement.

Key Learnings

- Modern hiring extends beyond resumes.
- Personal branding plays a significant role in career growth.
- ATS optimization improves visibility to recruiters.
- Targeted outreach increases networking effectiveness.
- AI can accelerate professional document creation.

Outcome

Created a complete career toolkit for future applications, networking, and leadership opportunities in education.

Tools Used

- Claude AI
- GitHub

Reflection

This exercise helped me understand how AI can support career development, personal branding, networking, and interview preparation.
